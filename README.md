# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

A comprehensive data analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, regional distributions, product categories, and key business metrics. Built to provide actionable insights for data-driven decision-making.


## 🚀 Key Features & KPIs

* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
  * **Total Revenue:** $1.57M
  * **Total Profit:** $175.26K
  * **Total Orders:** 3K
  * **Sales Growth:** +11.87%
  * **Profit Margin:** 11.19%
  * **Average Order Value (AOV):** $521.41

* **Interactive Filters / Slicers:**
  * Filter data by **Year**, **Quarter**, **Month**, **Region**, and **Category**.

* **In-Depth Visual Analytics:**
  * **Monthly Sales Trend:** Line chart showing sales revenue fluctuation across the year.
  * **Top 10 Selling Products:** Bar chart highlighting best-performing products by sales revenue.
  * **Low Performing Items:** Horizontal bar chart displaying products needing sales attention.
  * **Top 10 Sub-Categories:** Performance breakdown across sub-categories like Phones, Chairs, Binders, etc.
  * **Regional Sales Distribution:** Donut chart showcasing market share across West (33.37%), East (28.75%), Central (21.78%), and South (16.1%).
  * **Sales by Category:** Breakdown between Office Supplies (41.11%), Technology (30.05%), and Furniture (28.84%).
  * **Payment Mode Analysis:** Customer preference visualization for COD (42.62%), Online (35.38%), and Cards (21.99%).

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Top Payment Preference:** Cash on Delivery (COD) dominates with 42.62%, followed closely by Online payments (35.38%).
2. **Top Performing Region:** The **West** region leads in sales revenue at 33.37%.
3. **Category Breakdown:** **Office Supplies** generates the highest sales proportion (41.11%).
4. **Peak Month:** Sales reach their peak toward the end of the year in **December ($248K)**.
